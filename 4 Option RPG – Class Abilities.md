# 4 Option RPG – Class Abilities

Oct 28, 2026 · @Anthony Crockett

## Class system rules

Every class has five levels with one pick per level, and every specialization replaces the base class from Level 3 on. Fighter, Gladiator, and Pugilist are locked; every other class below is a first draft for review.

**Level structure (base class)**

| Level | Rank | Pick | Upgrades |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Apprentice | 1 of 2 Base abilities | Upgrades at Level 3, then Level 5 |
| 2 | Journeyman | 1 of 2 Minor abilities | Upgrades at Level 4 |
| 3 | Adept | 1 of 2 Major abilities | Upgrades at Level 5 |
| 4 | Veteran | 1 of 2 Minor abilities | None |
| 5 | Master | Mastery | None |

**Rules**

- An upgrade replaces the ability it upgrades and keeps everything the original did.
- Specializing (Level 3 or later) switches all current and future picks to the specialization. Abilities learned before specializing stay, and at each upgrade point the player takes either the base upgrade or the specialization's alternate.
- A specialization offer, once earned, stays open unless the player does something to lose it.
- Growth per class level: +level to Primary, plus +level free between Primary and Secondary (+15 and +15 by Level 5).
- Base classes are pure (Primary = Secondary). Each specialization adds its own Secondary.
- Speeds: Drawn (slow), Fair (normal), Swift (fast).
- Masteries and other big abilities reset through Rest, Sleep, Prayer where noted, an item, or a unique occupation ability.

**Luck and lucky classes**

- Class attributes count toward Luck but are exempt from the 100-point pair caps, so classes can push past natural limits.
- Rogue and Bard (and their specializations) add +1 Luck per class level; Jester adds +2 per level instead.
- Explorer is the one Luck class: LCK primary, no secondary, and all of its class growth goes straight to Luck (+30 by Level 5).
- Explorer entry needs 20 Luck from Natural attributes and racial modifiers, not gear.
- Explorer is a forced specialization: at Level 3 the character must become an Engineer or a Spelunker. There is no base Explorer beyond Level 2.

## New terms and decisions

These drafts add seven terms, and the design questions they raised are now decided.

**New terms**

| Term | Type | Draft meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hidden | State | Enemies can't target you directly; ends when you attack, unless an ability says otherwise |
| Evade | Action | Predict the attack's direction and avoid it entirely, instead of Blocking |
| Blinded | TNT | Disadvantage on attacks and Blocks |
| Poisoned | TNT | Damage each exchange for a set number of exchanges |
| Burning | TNT | Fire damage each exchange until put out |
| High ground | Position | Being above the enemy; Advantage on Head attacks |
| Companion | System | A bonded animal (Tamer) or NPC ally (Commander, Hired Blades) that acts each exchange |

**Decisions**

- **Allies are in scope, kept small.** A Tamer has one animal companion; a Commander leads up to two NPC allies (hired, recruited, or escorts). Allies take one basic action per exchange unless an ability directs them.
- **Prophet's Foretell stays** as the one exception to "information, not certainty." It is a miracle, once per Rest, and resets only through Prayer and Sleep.
- **Luck classes** follow the rules at the top: +1 Luck per level for Rogue and Bard, +2 for Jester, all growth to Luck for Explorer.
- **Mastery cost:** Exhausted is the standard cost, so players learn one rule. The exceptions are deliberate: Against All Odds, Combination, Miracle, Sun Stand Still, Unleash, Grand Machine, and Last Strength.
- **Overlaps are acceptable** because each sits in a different specialization: Jawbone deals full damage where Improvised doesn't; Taunting Wit (Enraged) and Provoke (TNT + TPT) work differently; Raise Stone is a terrain wall, not a Guard.
- **Specialization occupations are chosen for fit, not by attribute.** The table at the top is the working list.
- **Numbers wait for playtesting:** stamina restored, trap damage, and Poisoned and Burning damage per exchange.
- **Cleric stays the Faith base class name** for now.
